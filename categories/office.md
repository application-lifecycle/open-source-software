## Choosing the right office stack

- LibreOffice handles complex spreadsheets, long documents, and presentations offline on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- ONLYOFFICE Docs preserves Microsoft Word and Excel formatting reliably during live browser co-editing. Because ONLYOFFICE uses an open-core model, check Community Edition connection limits before deploying for large teams.
- CryptPad encrypts documents in the browser before they reach the server, making it practical for sensitive collaboration.
- Collabora Online embeds LibreOffice directly inside Nextcloud for teams that already self-host their file storage.
- For notes and internal wikis, Joplin fits encrypted Markdown notebooks, Logseq suits daily outliners, and AppFlowy targets teams moving off Notion.
