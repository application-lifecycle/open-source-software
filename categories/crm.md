# Open source CRM software

Open-source CRM platforms give sales and operations teams full ownership of customer records without per-seat SaaS pricing. Most options below run on Docker and PostgreSQL or MySQL/MariaDB.

## How to choose an open-source CRM

- Choose Twenty if your team is used to Linear or Notion and wants custom objects, Kanban pipelines, and a GraphQL API.
- Choose EspoCRM if you need IMAP email syncing, VoIP connectors, and granular role permissions out of the box.
- Choose SuiteCRM or Dolibarr when quotes, invoicing, contracts, and complex approval chains matter most.
- Choose Frappe CRM if you already run or plan to adopt ERPNext for inventory and accounting.
- Choose Monica for personal contact tracking and reminders.
